Support

Need help, found a bug, or have an idea that would make Chess Clarity better? We want to hear it.

Contact support

Email admin@overloaderstudio.com.

Useful things to include

  • What you were doing when the problem happened
  • Your iPhone or iPad model
  • Your iOS version
  • A screenshot if it helps explain the issue
  • For chess feedback: the position, move, or explanation that seems incorrect

Suggestions and improvements

Feature ideas, UI improvements, opening corrections, coaching suggestions, and other feedback are welcome.
